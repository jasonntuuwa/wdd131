Returns & Exchanges

If something isn't right, we'll make it right.

Our Policy

30-Day Window

Returns are accepted within 30 days of the delivery date. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and have all original tags attached.

How to Start a Return

Reach out through our Contact page with your order number. We'll send a prepaid return label and instructions within one business day.

Exchanges

Need a different size? Let us know in your return request and we'll ship the new size as soon as your return is scanned by the carrier, no need to wait for a refund first.

Refunds

Once your return is received and inspected, refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

Sale Items

Items marked as final sale are not eligible for return or exchange. This will be clearly noted on the product page at checkout.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, contact us within 7 days of delivery and we'll cover the cost of return shipping.
