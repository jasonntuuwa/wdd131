Our Fabric

Every Aethelon piece starts with the material. Here's what goes into each blend, and how to find your fit.

Built From the Blend Up

AeroFlex Performance Blend

A four-way stretch, moisture-wicking knit with mesh ventilation panels at the back and underarms. Used across our Activewear line, it's engineered to move with you through a full training session without holding onto sweat.

Structured Twill

A tightly woven, water-resistant twill that holds its shape and resists wrinkling, while still flexing at the shoulders and elbows. This is the fabric behind our Tailored Jackets, built to move straight from a morning session into a meeting.

Pima Cotton Blend

A long-staple cotton blend, softer and more durable than standard cotton, used throughout our Casual Essentials. It holds its shape wash after wash without losing the breathability that makes it comfortable all day.

Find Your Fit

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, in inches, and we'll recommend a starting size.
