Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to what we hear most, from sizing to shipping to membership perks.

Common Questions

What sizes do you carry?

We carry Small through XX-Large across every category. Not sure where you land? Use the fit calculator on our Fabric page to get a recommended starting size based on your chest measurement.

How do I join the Aethelon club?

Fill out the form on our Join the Club page. Members get early access to limited seasonal drops, member-only pricing, and first word on new fabric releases.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery on unworn items with tags attached. See our full Returns page for details on how to start a return or exchange.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently we ship within the United States and Canada. International shipping is on our roadmap; join the club to be notified when it becomes available.

How do I care for my Aethelon pieces?

Most activewear pieces are machine washable in cold water. Tailored jackets built from Structured Twill should be spot cleaned or dry cleaned to preserve their shape. Full care instructions are included on every product tag.

Still have a question?

Reach out through our Contact page and we'll get back to you.
