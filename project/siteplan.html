Aethelon Website Plan

Planning document for a premium, versatile men's clothing brand site.

Site Name

Aethelon

The name Aethelon fuses two ideas: Aethel, an Old English root meaning noble, and the athletic energy of a decathlon. It signals a brand built on classical, noble aesthetics paired with modern athletic performance — the same fusion the clothing line itself is built around.

Proposed domain: aethelon.com

Site Purpose

The Aethelon website establishes a premium digital storefront and lifestyle hub for the modern man. It provides high-quality, versatile menswear alongside curated style guides, technical fabric education, and an exclusive community membership program.

Key components required:

  • E-commerce storefront with dynamic product grids
  • Technical sizing and fit calculator
  • Fabric performance and education content
  • Interactive loyalty club sign-up portal

Scenarios

Scenario 1: Product Selection & Fit

"I am looking for a versatile jacket that transitions seamlessly from morning gym sessions to casual office meetings. Which fabrics in your collection offer the best blend of breathability and structure, and how do I ensure a tailored fit?"

Drives the need for an interactive fabric education section and a comprehensive sizing calculator.

Scenario 2: Community & Operations

"How can I join the Aethelon member club to get early access to limited seasonal clothing drops, and what is your return policy if the sizing isn't quite right?"

Drives the need for a prominent homepage loyalty registration form and highly visible footer links to FAQs and returns.

Color Schema

Colors follow the 60–30–10 rule for a balanced, premium retail feel.

Pentelic Marble

#F8F9FA — 60%

Dominant page background. A pristine, ultra-light cool gray that gives the storefront an open, luxury-retail atmosphere.

Olympic Charcoal

#1C1E21 — 30%

Structural focus. Used for headings, primary body text, and the navigation and footer blocks.

Aegean Blue

#005A9C — 10%

High-conversion accent, reserved for call-to-action buttons, active form fields, links, and hover states.

Typography

Montserrat — Display & Headings

Aa Bb Cc — SHOP THE COLLECTION

Used for major headers (h1, h2), all-caps navigation labels, and buttons. Conveys structured, athletic prestige.

Inter — Body & Interface

Aa Bb Cc — Premium, versatile menswear built for every part of your day.

High-readability interface font used for descriptive body text, form fields, text inputs, and data tables.
